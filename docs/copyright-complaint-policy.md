# SpiralDine Copyright & DMCA Complaint Procedure

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** October 2026  
**Governing Laws:** Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021 (India), Section 52(1)(b) Indian Copyright Act, 1957, and Digital Millennium Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. § 512).

---

## 1. Intermediary Status & Policy Statement

SpiralDine (operated by Spiral World / RestaurantOS) operates as an online platform and SaaS intermediary. Restaurants, food operators, and third-party users upload menus, descriptions, culinary photographs, and brand assets.

SpiralDine respects the intellectual property rights of others and strictly prohibits the posting of infringing materials. Under the Information Technology Rules, 2021 and 17 U.S.C. § 512, SpiralDine implements this expeditious **Notice-and-Takedown Procedure** for copyright owners and authorized agents.

## 3. Submitting a Notice of Infringement (Takedown Notice)

To be legally effective, an infringement notice must include the following information:

1. **Identification of the Protected Work**: A clear description or digital copy of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed (e.g., specific food photograph, menu branding, literary text).
2. **Identification of Infringing Content**: The exact URL, restaurant page link, or specific mobile application screen displaying the infringing material, with sufficient detail to enable SpiralDine to locate it on our servers.
3. **Complainant Contact Information**: Full name, legal entity name (if applicable), physical address, telephone number, and official email address.
4. **Statement of Good Faith**: A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the disputed use of the material is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law (such as fair use).
5. **Statement of Accuracy & Authority**: A statement under penalty of perjury that the information in the notification is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on the copyright owner’s behalf.
6. **Signature**: A physical or authorized electronic signature of the copyright owner or person authorized to act on their behalf.

Upon receiving a valid and complete infringement notice:

```
[Infringement Notice Received]

               │
        ┌──────┴──────┐
     Valid         Deficient
        │             │
        ▼             ▼
[Expeditiously Disable]  [Request Clarification from Complainant]
[Access / Hide Asset]
        │
        ▼
[Notify Uploader (Restaurant Owner)]
        │
        ▼
[Provide 14-Day Counter-Notice Window]
```

1. **Expeditious Removal**: SpiralDine temporarily disables public access to the disputed asset (or replaces it with a generic placeholder icon).
2. **Notification to Uploader**: SpiralDine immediately notifies the restaurant owner who posted the asset, providing a copy of the takedown notice.
3. **Repeat Infringer Policy**: Accounts of restaurant operators or users found to be repeat intentional infringers will face service termination and platform banning.

## 5. Counter-Notification Procedure

If a restaurant owner believes in good faith that the removed material was removed by mistake or misidentification (e.g. they own the rights or hold a valid commercial license), they may submit a Counter-Notification containing:

1. **Identification of Removed Material**: Description and original location of the removed content.
2. **Statement Under Penalty of Perjury**: A statement that the user has a good faith belief that the material was removed or disabled as a result of mistake or misidentification.
3. **Consent to Jurisdiction**: A statement consenting to the jurisdiction of the competent courts in the jurisdiction where the user or SpiralDine is located.
4. **Contact Details & Signature**: Full name, address, telephone, email, and signature.

Upon receipt of an effective counter-notification, SpiralDine forwards it to the original complainant. If the complainant does not file a court action within 10 to 14 business days, SpiralDine may restore the disabled material.
