# Inventory, Vendor Directory & Purchase Order Architecture Audit

## 1. Executive Summary

This audit assesses the current state of SpiralDine's Inventory Management system, examining existing data models, UI components, transactional services, multi-tenant isolation, and communication capabilities. The goal is to establish safe integration points for extending the existing Inventory Automation suite with a **Vendor Directory** and an end-to-end **Purchase Order & Goods Receiving Workflow**, avoiding redundant or duplicate subsystems.

---

## 2. Existing Architecture & Inventory Subsystems

### 2.1 UI Navigation & Screens
* **Primary Desktop Route**: `/owner/inventory` and `/owner/inventory/purchase-orders` (rendered by `src/apps/owner/pages/OwnerInventoryManager.tsx`).
* **Existing Tabs in `OwnerInventoryManager.tsx`**:
  1. `overview`: KPI cards (Kitchen Stock Value, Low Stock Alerts, Active Suppliers, Waste Loss Value).
  2. `ingredients`: Ingredients list table with category filters, search, and Add/Edit/Delete modals.
  3. `recipes`: Recipe mappings linking menu items to ingredients with yield and waste percentages.
  4. `movements`: Stock Movements Log (audit trail of purchases, consumption, adjustments, waste, restocks).
  5. `suppliers`: Basic Supplier list (Name, Phone, Email, Address, Delivery Days, Rating, Terms).
  6. `lowstock`: Low stock warnings table with stock health badges (`healthy`, `low`, `critical`, `out_of_stock`).
  7. `expiry`: Expiry monitor identifying batches nearing expiration.
  8. `waste`: Waste logging modal and historical loss ledger.
  9. `suggestions`: Replenishment suggestions auto-generated by the stock health engine.
* **Mobile Owner Inventory**: `src/apps/owner/mobile/pages/MobileInventory.tsx` (responsive cards and quick stock adjustments).

### 2.2 Domain Data Model (`src/shared/domain/inventory/types.ts`)
* **`IStockIngredient`**: ID, name, category, unit (`kg`, `g`, `liters`, `ml`, `pieces`, `packs`), currentStock, minimumStock, maximumStock, reorderLevel, supplierId, supplierName, purchaseCost (in cents), storageLocation, expiryDate, status.
* **`ISupplier`**: ID, name, phone, email, address, suppliedIngredientIds, deliveryTimeDays, rating, companyName, gstNumber, paymentTerms, deliverySchedule.
* **`IStockMovement`**: ID, ingredientId, ingredientName, quantity, unit, previousStock, newStock, type (`purchase`, `consumption`, `adjustment`, `waste`, `spoilage`, `refund_restock`, `cancellation_restock`), reason, submittedBy, submittedByName, performedByRole, tenantId, timestamp.
* **`IPurchaseSuggestion`**: ID, ingredientId, ingredientName, recommendedQuantity, unit, supplierId, supplierName, estimatedCost, priority, createdAt, status (`pending`, `ordered`, `completed`).
* **`IRecipe` & `IRecipeIngredient`**: Yield quantities and ingredient breakdown for menu items.
* **`IWasteLog`**: Quantities lost, reason, and estimated monetary cost.

### 2.3 Authoritative Backend Services (`src/shared/services/inventoryService.ts`)
The existing `inventoryService` provides battle-tested, transaction-safe operations:
* `receiveStock(tenantId, ingredientId, options)`: Uses a Firestore transaction to update `currentStock`, recomputes stock status, updates `purchaseCost` and `supplierId`, and appends an immutable `IStockMovement` (`type: 'purchase'`).
* `adjustStock(...)`: Adjusts stock level and records `type: 'adjustment'`.
* `recordWaste(...)`: Deducts stock and records `type: 'waste'`.
* `deductStockForOrder(...)` / `restockStockForOrder(...)`: Order-based consumption and refund restocking.
* `deductBatchServings(...)` / `restockBatchServings(...)`: Cooked batch management.
* `calculateStockStatus(...)`: Evaluates `healthy`, `low`, `critical`, or `out_of_stock` based on `minimumStock` and `reorderLevel`.

### 2.4 Multi-Tenant Context & Authorization
* **Tenant & Branch Scope**:
  * Root Firestore path: `/restaurants/{tenantId}/`
  * Staff access governed by `useAuth()` (`tenantId`, `branchId`, `role`).
  * Firestore rules (`firestore.rules` lines 518–557) enforce:
    * `/restaurants/{tenantId}/inventory/*`: `isTenantStaff(tenantId)`
    * `/restaurants/{tenantId}/stockMovements/*`: `allow read, create: if isTenantStaff(tenantId); allow update, delete: if false;` (Immutable audit trail).
    * Subcollections like `suppliers`, `purchaseOrders`, `supplierPrices`, `goodsReceipts` fall under the tenant catch-all rule which restricts reads to `isTenant(tenantId)` and writes to `isTenantStaff(tenantId)`. Cross-tenant data leakage is structurally impossible.

## 3. Verified Functionality vs. Gaps & Deficiencies

| Feature Area | Current Status | Verified Functionality | Identified Deficiencies / Gaps |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Supplier Directory** | Basic | Basic supplier creation, editing, contact details, rating. | Missing active/inactive toggle, tax/GSTIN validation, delivery lead time presets, full purchase order history link, multi-branch scoping. |
| **Supplier-Ingredient Pricing** | Incomplete | Ingredient points to a single `supplierId` and `purchaseCost`. | No many-to-many supplier-ingredient pricing catalog, no min order quantities, no lead times per supplier, no price history or effective dates. |
| **Purchase Orders** | Incomplete | Route exists (`/owner/inventory/purchase-orders`), but falls back to `suggestions` tab without dedicated PO workflow. | No dedicated PO generation, no PO status lifecycle (`DRAFT`, `SENT`, `CONFIRMED`, `PARTIALLY_RECEIVED`, `DELIVERED`, `CANCELLED`), no PO line items snapshot. |
| **Goods Receiving** | Partial | `inventoryService.receiveStock()` exists for direct ingredient receiving. | No structured PO receipt screen matching PO line items against incoming vendor invoices, no partial delivery support with outstanding balances. |
| **Replenishment Suggestions** | Partial | Suggestions generated on low stock; "Complete" button directly adds stock and deletes suggestion. | Does not convert suggestions into draft Purchase Orders for formal supplier procurement. |
| **Document Sharing** | Incomplete | Basic email sending service via Resend exists for staff invitations. | No WhatsApp prefilled purchase order message formatting, no email PO dispatch with itemized breakdown. |

## 4. Architecture & Safe Integration Strategy

To maintain complete backward compatibility and avoid duplicate systems:

1. **Extend Existing Types (`src/shared/domain/inventory/types.ts`)**:
   * Enhance `ISupplier` with `isActive`, `taxId` / `gstin`, `notes`, `contactPerson`, `branchIds`.
   * Add `ISupplierPrice`: many-to-many catalog mapping `(supplierId, ingredientId)` with `unitPrice`, `purchaseUnit`, `minOrderQty`, `leadTimeDays`, `isPreferred`, `effectiveDate`.
   * Add `IPurchaseOrder`: `id`, `poNumber`, `tenantId`, `branchId`, `supplierId`, `supplierName`, `items` (snapshot of ingredient name, unit, qty, unitPrice, lineTotal, receivedQty), `status`, `subtotal`, `taxAmount`, `grandTotal`, `expectedDeliveryDate`, `deliveryAddress`, `notes`, `auditHistory`.
   * Add `IGoodsReceipt`: `id`, `poId`, `poNumber`, `tenantId`, `invoiceNumber`, `receivedItems` (ingredientId, qtyReceived, qtyRejected, notes), `receivedBy`, `receivedAt`.

2. **Extend `inventoryService.ts`**:
   * Add `processPurchaseOrderReceipt(tenantId, poId, receiptData, user)`:
     * Executes inside a Firestore transaction.
     * Validates that received quantities do not cause double-counting.
     * Invokes the existing stock addition and records corresponding `IStockMovement` records linked to `poNumber`.
     * Calculates updated PO status (`PARTIALLY_RECEIVED` vs `DELIVERED`).
     * Prevents duplicate receipts using idempotency tokens.

3. **Enhance `OwnerInventoryManager.tsx`**:
   * Add two integrated sub-tabs to `navTabs`:
     * `purchase-orders`: Purchase Orders management (Drafts, Sent, Confirmed, Receiving, History).
     * Enhance `suppliers`: Vendor Directory with active status, associated ingredients & pricing catalog, order history.
   * Connect `suggestions` tab: "Create Draft PO" button that bundles replenishment items into a new PO.

4. **Add Sharing Capabilities**:
   * **WhatsApp**: `https://wa.me/{phone}?text={encodedPO}` with structured PO invoice layout.
   * **Email**: Prepares formatted plain-text and HTML purchase order specifications for direct dispatch or desktop mail client integration.

5. **Security & Rules Compliance**:
   * All records reside under `/restaurants/{tenantId}/`.
   * Strict tenant scoping ensures multi-tenant boundary compliance.
